# Badge Light store copy

## Title

Badge Light

## Short description

Survive a three-minute closing shift in a dark mall. Sweep back haunted mannequins, scan threats from range, and keep your badge light alive.

## Long description

The mall is closed. The displays are moving. Your badge light is fading.

Badge Light is a fast, top-down survival game about making it through one impossible closing shift. Fight cracked mannequins and possessed shopping carts with a close-range broom or limited-ammo price scanner. Collect snacks and batteries, reactivate the mall's directory kiosks, and manage the shrinking circle of light around you.

Every run lasts at most three minutes, but the mall gets more dangerous as opening time approaches. Power all four kiosks, build a high score, and make it to the end of the shift.

## Feature bullets

- A complete arcade run in three minutes.
- Two weapons with different ranges and resource tradeoffs.
- Two enemy behaviors: relentless mannequins and telegraphed charging carts.
- A draining-light system tied to exploration and kiosk management.
- Pickups, escalating pressure, score chasing, and a persistent best score.
- Keyboard and automatic touch controls, plus pause and mute options.
- No download, login, or account required to play.

## Controls

- Move: Arrow keys or WASD
- Attack: Space
- Switch weapon: X
- Activate nearby kiosk: Z
- Feed a battery to an active kiosk: C
- Pause: P
- Mute: M
- Touch: directional pad, dedicated weapon buttons, and USE near a kiosk
